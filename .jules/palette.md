## 2024-05-30 - Actionable Error Messages for State Reset
**Learning:** In text-based interfaces where context bounds are hidden (like Discord bots with conversational memory), users often get stuck when the agent context overflows or gets corrupted without knowing how to unblock themselves.
**Action:** Always provide explicit, actionable suggestions (like command hints such as `!clear`) within error messages to empower users to reset their state and recover from hidden errors gracefully.
